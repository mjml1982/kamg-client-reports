⚠️ Platform Note — Monday & Friday Volume
The strategy called for half-volume on Mondays and Fridays. However, when using Lemlist's timezone-based scheduling (required to send in each prospect's local time), the platform does not allow setting different sending quotas per day of the week. As a result, Monday and Friday are set to the same volume as Tuesday–Thursday. This is a platform constraint, not a configuration oversight.
